Why Shippers Trust Isunza

We're not a load board — we're your partner. Here's what that actually looks like.

A Real Person Who Knows You

Staying boutique was a deliberate choice. Instead of scaling up and spreading ourselves thin, we've kept our team small so every shipment gets hands-on attention — not a fraction of it. There's no maze of departments or chains of emails to chase down about your freight. Just one accountable point of contact, ready to respond immediately. Because to us, a load isn't something to check off a list — it's a chance to show you who we are, earn your trust, and become a real, lasting part of your transportation network.

Carrier Accountability

Every carrier in our network is vetted and verified through our partners at Descartes — checking FMCSA authority, CSA scores, and insurance minimums before they ever touch your freight. We've built rigorous onboarding standards for every driver who picks up a load for us, and we can proudly say we've never had a stolen or missing load. As a boutique broker, we're able to speak directly with every driver on every load — real communication that keeps your freight, and your peace of mind, on track from pickup to delivery. We don't cut corners on compliance. That's why shippers trust Isunza with their freight.

Real-Time Accountability

We maintain a 96%+ tracking compliance rate across our customer base — because we're compatible with all major tracking platforms. Compliance is monitored hourly, so updates are always known in real-time. Whether you need check calls, GPS updates, or ELD integration, we work within your existing systems — not around them — so visibility is never a negotiation. Our TMS connects via EDI and API — integration isn't a hurdle, it's a given.

Partner With Us as a Carrier

Tired of brokers who don't pay detention and layover, or who leave you waiting to get your invoice factored after delivery? Whether you're running one truck or a national fleet, we believe consistent, fair pay isn't optional — it's the baseline. If you run a clean operation and show up when you say you will, we want to work with you — consistently.

We work with owner-operators, small fleets, and national carriers alike. Onboarding is straightforward, our credit scores and days-to-pay are among the highest in the industry, and we build real working relationships, not one-load transactions.

About Isunza Logistics

Isunza Logistics Inc. is a full-truckload freight brokerage headquartered in Orland Park, Illinois, with over 10 years of experience in the industry. We operate across all major equipment types and lanes — from temperature-controlled reefer to flatbed and oversized — serving shippers who need reliable execution and carriers who value consistent volume and on-time pay.

What sets us apart isn't a technology platform or a load board — it's accountability. Every account gets a dedicated rep who knows the freight, knows the lane, and knows the driver. That rep is your single point of contact from booking through delivery, so nothing gets lost in translation.

We're built for long-term partnerships, not transactional volume. Every carrier we work with is vetted through MyCarrierPackets and Carrier411 before they haul for us, because the quality of our carrier network is a direct reflection of the service we deliver to shippers.
