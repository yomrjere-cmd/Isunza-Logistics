What We Move

Six equipment types. One point of contact. Whatever you're shipping and wherever it's going, we have the solution.

❄️ Temperature Controlled

Reefer

Perishable goods, pharmaceuticals, and temperature-sensitive freight moved in properly maintained reefer units with precise monitoring throughout transit.

🚚 Standard Freight

Dry Van

The workhorse of freight. We move boxed, palletized, and general dry goods across all lanes — regional, long-haul, and coast-to-coast.

⚒️ Heavy Haul

Flatbed & Oversized

Machinery, structural steel, building materials, and oversize loads handled with proper permitting, rigging, and routing coordination.

⚡ Expedited

Sprinter Vans & Hot Shots

Time-critical freight that can't wait for a full truck. Fast, direct point-to-point delivery for urgent shipments with a tight timeline.

🔌 Flexible

Power Only

You manage the trailer, we provide the truck. Power-only service for shippers who run their own trailer pools and need reliable tractor capacity.

🔖 Drop & Hook

Drop Trailer

Streamlined dock operations with drop trailer solutions — reducing dwell time, improving throughput, and keeping your yard moving on schedule.

About Isunza Logistics

Isunza Logistics Inc. is a full-truckload freight brokerage headquartered in Orland Park, Illinois, with over 10 years of experience in the industry. We operate across all major equipment types and lanes — from temperature-controlled reefer to flatbed and oversized — serving shippers who need reliable execution and carriers who value consistent volume and on-time pay.

What sets us apart isn't a technology platform or a load board — it's accountability. Every account gets a dedicated rep who knows the freight, knows the lane, and knows the driver. That rep is your single point of contact from booking through delivery, so nothing gets lost in translation.

We're built for long-term partnerships, not transactional volume. Every carrier we work with is vetted through MyCarrierPackets and Carrier411 before they haul for us, because the quality of our carrier network is a direct reflection of the service we deliver to shippers.
